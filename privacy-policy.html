Moove Out - Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026 · Tin Compass Studio

The short version

Moove Out has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress, clovers and settings stay on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play or the App Store; those services handle some data as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, cowbells, clovers, boosters and herd book.
  • Your settings (music, sound, vibration, reduce motion, show paths).
  • A record of purchases already delivered, so nothing is granted twice.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Ads

Moove Out uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. Before any ad loads, Google's consent form asks for your choice where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK); you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options. On iOS, the game asks once for permission to track; declining changes only how relevant ads are.

AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which process your payment. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase happened, so the game can deliver it. A refunded purchase is taken back (for example, ads return if Remove ads is refunded).

Children

Moove Out is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights and deleting your data

We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the game stores, uninstall it or clear its storage. For data held by Google for ads, use Settings → Privacy options or your device's advertising settings.
